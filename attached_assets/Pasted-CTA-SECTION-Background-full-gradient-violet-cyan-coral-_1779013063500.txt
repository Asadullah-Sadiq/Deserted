FORMS (Modal-based, 3 types):

MODAL DESIGN:
- Overlay: rgba(0,0,0,0.8) backdrop with blur
- Modal: Card style, max-width 640px, scrollable
- Header: title + close button (X)

FORM 1 — Start a Project (3 Steps):
Step 1 "About You":
  - First Name*, Last Name*, Email*, Phone, Company*, Role (dropdown)*
  Role options: CEO, CTO, Product Manager, Developer, Designer, Other

Step 2 "Your Project":
  - Services Needed* (multi-select chips, not dropdown):
    [AI/ML] [Web Dev] [Cloud] [Mobile] [Security] [Data]
  - Project Description* (textarea, 4 rows)
  - Project Type* (dropdown): MVP, Enhancement, Migration, Consulting, Retainer

Step 3 "Budget & Timeline":
  - Budget Range* (radio cards):
    [$1K–$10K] [$10K–$25K] [$25K–$50K] [$50K–$100K] [$100K+]
  - Timeline* (radio cards):
    [ASAP] [1–3 Months] [3–6 Months] [Flexible]
  - How did you hear about us? (dropdown)

STEP INDICATOR: 
- 3 dots/circles at top, active = filled gradient, done = checkmark
- Progress bar underneath

FORM 2 — Schedule a Call:
Fields: Full Name*, Email*, Company, Topic* (dropdown), 
        Preferred Date* (date picker), Preferred Time* (time picker), 
        Notes (textarea)

FORM 3 — Partnership:
Fields: Name*, Email*, Company*, Website, Partnership Type* (dropdown),
        Company Size (dropdown), Proposal* (textarea)

- React Hook Form + Zod validation
